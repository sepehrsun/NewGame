# Comet Leap – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 24, 2026_

Comet Leap ("the app") is an offline game.

**The app does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal or device data.**

- No account or sign-in is required.
- The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
- The app does not request any runtime permissions and does not access the internet.
- Your game progress (best score, collected stars, unlocked skins and settings) is stored only on your device. If you have Android backup enabled, Android may include this small file in your personal Google device backup, which is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device.

## Children
The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.

## Contact
Questions: please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository, or use the developer contact email shown on the Google Play store listing.
