☄️ Comet Leap is a one-tap arcade game about timing, gravity and nerve.

Your comet circles a planet. Tap to break orbit and leap into the dark — if you reach the next world's orbit you're captured and safe… for now. Orbits decay, so don't linger!

★ ONE-TAP, ENDLESS
Easy to learn, impossible to put down. Every planet is generated on the fly, so no two runs are the same.

★ PERFECT LEAPS
Graze an orbit at just the right angle for a PERFECT capture. Chain them for combo multipliers and huge scores.

★ REAL GRAVITY
Planets bend your path. Use them — or get flung into the void.

★ DANGERS OF DEEP SPACE
Moving planets, orbiting asteroid belts and hungry black holes appear the higher you climb.

★ POWER-UPS
Shields that save you from one mistake, and magnets that pull every star your way.

★ 8 COMET SKINS
Collect stars and unlock Glacier, Toxin, Solar, Void, the colour-shifting Prism and more.

★ DAILY GIFT
Come back each day for free stars.

★ FAIR & PRIVATE
No ads. No in-app purchases. No tracking. No internet needed. Plays perfectly offline.

★ TINY DOWNLOAD
Under 100 KB — every planet, sound and the soundtrack are generated by code.

Available in English and Persian (فارسی).
